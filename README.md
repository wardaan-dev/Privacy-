    <p>This policy explains how <strong>Wardaan Dev</strong> ("we", "us") handles information when you message a WhatsApp assistant that we build and operate for businesses, such as the <strong>Car Rental Agent</strong> assistant. We are based in Sanghar, Sindh, Pakistan.</p>

    <h3>What we collect</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name</strong>, so the assistant can reply to you.</li>
      <li><strong>The messages you send</strong> to the business's WhatsApp number.</li>
      <li><strong>Booking details you choose to share</strong>, such as your name, the service or car you want, and your preferred date and time.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>We do not collect payment card details, passwords, or any information you do not send to the assistant yourself.</p>

    <h3>How we use it</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>To answer your questions about the business (prices, timing, location, services).</li>
      <li>To record booking requests so the business owner can confirm them.</li>
      <li>To remember the context of your conversation so you do not have to repeat yourself.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.</p>

    <h3>Services that process your data</h3>
    <p>To provide the assistant, your messages pass through these services:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta)</strong> – delivers messages between you and the business.</li>
      <li><strong>n8n</strong> – the automation platform that runs the assistant.</li>
      <li><strong>An AI language model provider</strong> (currently Groq) – reads your message to write a reply.</li>
      <li><strong>Google Sheets</strong> – stores the business's FAQ and booking requests.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.</p>

    <h3>How long we keep it</h3>
    <p>Booking requests are kept for as long as the business needs them to manage bookings. Conversation context used by the assistant is short-term. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).</p>

    <h3>Security</h3>
    <p>Access to the systems that store your information is limited to us and the business you are contacting. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.</p>

    <h3>Children</h3>
    <p>This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.</p>
